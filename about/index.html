Dr. Emma Pollard

Obstetrics & Gynecology

Dr. Pollard is an Obstetrician and Gynaecologist at Water St. ObGyn in Cambridge, Ontario. Originally from Brampton, Ontario, she completed her medical degree at McMaster University before pursuing residency training in Obstetrics and Gynaecology at Dalhousie University.

Dr. Pollard is committed to providing compassionate, evidence-based care throughout every stage of life. Her clinical interests include contraception, gender-affirming care, menopause care, and the management of heavy menstrual bleeding. She offers comprehensive gynaecologic care and performs the full spectrum of laparoscopic, open, and vaginal surgery, tailoring treatment plans to each patient's individual needs and goals.

Outside of medicine, Dr. Pollard enjoys reading, hiking, and spending time with her two young sons. She values building meaningful relationships with her patients and strives to create a welcoming, respectful environment where every patient feels heard and supported.
