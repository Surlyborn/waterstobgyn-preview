Parking, transit & access

Free parking at the building. There is a surface lot at the door and an overflow lot, with no charge to park.

The building is fully accessible, with a wheelchair ramp at the entrance and an elevator to our office on the sixth floor.

By bus: Grand River Transit's Ainslie St Terminal is a short walk away, served by ION Bus 302 and several local Cambridge routes.

Referred to our clinic?

Once you have a referral from your doctor, midwife, or nurse practitioner, you can complete our intake form before your first visit.
