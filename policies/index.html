Clinic policies

Policies, in plain language.

The office policies of Dr. Mendlowitz and Dr. Pollard, as written by the clinic. If anything is unclear, call the office and we will walk you through it.

P.01

Clinic hours

Office hours are usually weekdays from 9am to 4pm. Earlier or later appointments are only offered for urgent situations, to accommodate you if the doctors' schedules are otherwise filled during normal working hours.

Our medical office assistants answer telephone calls during normal working hours when a clinic is taking place. On days the doctors are working in the hospital, they will check voicemails and emails. Non-urgent concerns should be responded to within 2 business days.

P.02

Appointment times

We do our best to keep to your scheduled appointment time as closely as possible. Delays may occur if patients need more time to address their concerns. The doctors will do their best to ensure we can address your concerns when it is your turn.

P.03

Late appointments

Patients arriving late for appointments create a significant barrier to clinics running on time. To respect patients who have taken care to arrive on time, we may not be able to accommodate you if you attend late.

  • For new consultations, we are unable to guarantee you will be seen if you attend 10 minutes late, or on time but without intake forms completed.
  • As follow-up appointments are booked for a shorter amount of time, your appointment may have to be rescheduled if you are 5 minutes late.
  • As prenatal follow-ups are time sensitive, you may be asked to wait until the end of clinic to be seen.
P.04

Missed appointments & rebooking fees

Please do not attend the office if you are unwell. If you have a cough, fever, sore throat, or other infectious symptoms, we are happy to rebook your appointment, even on short notice. There is no penalty for rebooking in the case of illness or family emergency.

For new consultations, appointments are confirmed via telephone with a follow-up email confirmation. Reminders are sent via Ocean by email 1 week before your appointment, and by text (if a cell number is provided) 2 days before. Please complete your intake online via the Ocean link, or arrive early to your appointment. Appointment confirmations are also sent to the referring physician.

If a new consultation is cancelled within 2 business days of the appointment, a rebooking fee of $135 will be charged before a new appointment is scheduled. Reminders are not routinely offered for follow-up appointments. For follow-up appointments, we charge $50 to rebook after 2 missed appointments. Late cancellations, or attending late as outlined above, are considered missed appointments and a rebooking fee will also apply.

Of note: if an email is provided to our clinic, failing to check an email confirmation is not considered a valid reason for missing a confirmed appointment.

Patients who are not covered by provincial or federal health plans (such as OHIP or Interim Federal Health) will be required to pay privately at OMA recommended rates. Patients who are eligible for OHIP but have invalid coverage (for example, expired) will be required to pay for appointments, or may rebook after they have renewed their healthcare coverage. Please ensure your OHIP is valid before your appointment.

Uninsured fees are payable via cash, e-transfer, or Square. Service fees apply for Square transactions.

P.06

Masking, support people & children

Our office sees immunocompromised and vulnerable patients. Our learned experience is that despite our requests for sick patients not to attend appointments, patients often still attend with respiratory symptoms. We kindly ask you to wear a mask if you have any cold or flu symptoms. We are happy to provide you with complimentary medical-grade masks.

One support person is allowed per appointment. We are happy for you to bring well children to your appointment. Please do not bring your sick child to an appointment. Knowingly bringing an infectious child to the office is considered a breach of trust and grounds for dismissal from the practice.

P.07

Abusive behaviour

We aim to provide a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment. Abuse toward the physicians or office assistants, which may include but is not limited to raised voices, insults, or threats, will not be tolerated and is grounds for dismissal from the medical practice as per CPSO policy.

We are happy for you to take pictures of the office or art, but there is absolutely no recording of office members without expressed consent.

Questions about a policy?

Call the office and we will walk you through it.
