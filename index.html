What to expect at your visit

Your body. Your decisions.

Care here begins with respect for your bodily autonomy. Nothing happens without your understanding and your consent.

Your first appointment

We talk through the reason for your referral or your gynecologic concern, and your medical history. It may include a physical exam and a discussion of options for diagnosis and treatment.

One step at a time

Reaching a diagnosis and a treatment plan can take more than one appointment, depending on your individual needs.

Comfort matters

If an exam calls for extra pain relief, we plan for it, sometimes with a separate appointment, so nothing is rushed.
